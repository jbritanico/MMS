Green tagReady for service.
Yellow tagUnder maintenance (repair work in progress).
Red tagNot ready for service.

Points to confirm

1. Decisions given by phone2. Tag colour after a Moderate fault is reviewed
What the manual says Moderate faults are reviewed by the Operations Coordinator or Maintenance Supervisor. Major/Critical faults are reviewed by the FSM or Maintenance Manager.
Section 6.1.5, Table 3, pages 11–12		 For a Moderate fault: “Plan rectification and monitor the condition until repaired.”
Table 3, page 12
Every unit must always show Green, Yellow or Red.
Section 8.1, page 26
Guidance required on What happens when the reviewer cannot log in to MMS and gives the decision by phone. Which colour the unit shows while the Moderate repair is waiting to be done.
What MMS does today 1. The Job Supervisor on site enters the decision on the reviewer’s behalf and records that it was given by phone.
2. The decision is marked Provisional (temporary). Work can go ahead straight away.
3. The report cannot be closed until the reviewer logs in to MMS and confirms the decision personally.		 1. A Moderate fault does not stop the unit working, so it is not Red.
2. No repair work has started yet, so it is not Yellow.
3. Once the reviewer has looked at the fault, the unit is tagged Green (ready for service).
4. The planned repair stays open in MMS and is tracked until it is done.
Question Is it acceptable to allow decisions by phone in this way? Is it correct that a unit with only a Moderate fault shows Green while the repair is planned?
