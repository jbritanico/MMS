Dear Kamil,

Following the issue of the Maintenance Management Manual Rev 02 and the MMS Dashboard Visualization deck, we reviewed the MMS application against both and prepared a work plan. MR-I goes live in November, with MR-II and MR-III following in the succeeding months. The summary, the full work plan, the dashboard chart list and the decisions we need from you are below.

In short, the MR-I escalation workflow already follows the three-level severity table in the Manual. The gaps for MR-I are the Yellow tag, the Red-tag MOC exemption, closure and approval rules by inspection stage, and the mapping of the access tiers to application roles. Breakdown Maintenance, the MR-II and MR-III reports, the compliance reports and MR-III planning are not built yet. Of the 18 charts in the dashboard deck, 5 can ship with MR-I, 8 more with MR-II and 5 with MR-III, because the later charts depend on data that only those releases record.
