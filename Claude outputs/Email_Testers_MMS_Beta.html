Good day, and thank you for helping test the new MMS. You will test the offline MR-I workflow on your laptop and tell me what works, what is wrong and what is unclear. Your feedback will be used to improve MMS before public testing.

About the new MMS: MMS is Sprint's maintenance system for the whole life of each piece of equipment: the asset register, MR-I, MR-II and MR-III inspections, breakdown repairs, fault review and approval, and Red/Green equipment tags. It also tracks running hours, distance and calendar days to forecast when maintenance is due, and gives management dashboards on reliability, defects and compliance. It is built offline first: it runs on your laptop and keeps working with no internet at site, with your data stored securely on the laptop and shared with the central system once the online sync is ready.

How to start

#Step
1Create a folder Documents\MMS Beta and save both attached HTML files in it. Do not move or rename the testing form later: your results are kept with it.
2Open MR-I Process.html (double-click; it opens in Edge or Chrome). Read the 15 steps and try the questions.
3Run the installer. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", press More info then Run anyway. Choose "Install for me only".
4Open MMS-Testing Form.html. At the top, type your name and choose your site.
5Follow the tests from 1 to 35, top to bottom. Each test shows which MMS user to sign in as, the steps, what you should see, and a screenshot. Press Mark as done when finished.

Good to know

  • To switch to another user from the table above, use the arrows beside the name on the MMS start screen.
  • No internet is needed for testing.
  • Use the same browser each time, and do not clear its browsing data. If your results ever disappear, use Send results → Restore from a results file with your last exported file. It is a good idea to export a backup copy from time to time while you test.
  • Beta data is for testing only and is not the official record. Continue the current process as normal.
  • If you cannot continue (a "Blocker"), report it and send me a message.

Please try to work things out on your own. This test also checks how easy MMS is to use without training. If a screen, button or message is confusing, or you are not sure what to do next, please report it in Report an issue, even if you work it out in the end. That feedback is as useful as reporting errors.
